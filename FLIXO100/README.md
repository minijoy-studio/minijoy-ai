# FLIXO 100 AI — Prototype

## What has been created
- A registry of 100 specialist **roles**, grouped into five teams of 20.
- A small task-routing prototype that suggests which team should handle a task.
- Human approval is required before any external action.

## Important status
This is **not** 100 running autonomous AI agents, not a deployed service, and not an income-generating bot. The registry is a blueprint. The router is a basic keyword-based prototype; it does not call an AI model, create customers, send messages, trade crypto, or transfer funds.

## Teams
1. Video & Animation — 20 roles
2. Customer Discovery & Sales — 20 roles
3. Design & Advertising — 20 roles
4. Content & Social Media — 20 roles
5. Operations, Finance & Risk — 20 roles

## Safe build sequence
1. Validate the role registry and task router.
2. Connect one AI model to the central dispatcher.
3. Build one real workflow: create a sample video offer, draft outreach, and prepare a human-reviewed proposal.
4. Add customer approval, privacy controls, rate limits, cost tracking, and audit logs.
5. Test with real paying customers before scaling.
6. Only then add more model-backed agents.

## Money and crypto safety
- No guaranteed daily income. A target such as $5 per role per day is a business goal, not a forecast.
- Do not collect card numbers, CVV, PINs, passwords, or SMS codes.
- Never store exchange credentials or private keys in this repository.
- No autonomous trading, transfers, purchases, or payouts. Any financial action requires explicit human approval and a compliant payment provider.
- Start with customer-funded digital services rather than speculative trading.

## Current deployment
This prototype is committed to GitHub only. It is not deployed to Cloudflare and is not connected to a public FLIXO website.
