# FLIXO 1000 AI — Global Role Registry

## Scope
FLIXO 1000 AI defines 1,000 specialist role blueprints across 10 worldwide service divisions. Each division contains 10 task families mapped to 10 customer market segments (10 × 10 = 100 roles per division; 10 divisions = 1,000 roles).

## Divisions
1. Video & Animation — 100 roles
2. Global Sales & Customer Acquisition — 100 roles
3. Graphic Design & Advertising — 100 roles
4. Social Media & Publishing — 100 roles
5. E-commerce & Online Stores — 100 roles
6. Writing & Documentation — 100 roles
7. Translation & Localization — 100 roles
8. Education & Learning — 100 roles
9. Audio & Voice Services — 100 roles
10. Websites, Analytics & Operations — 100 roles

## Worldwide approach
- Initial working languages in the role registry: English, Russian, and Kyrgyz.
- Additional languages and region-specific compliance can be added after selecting target markets.
- Each role is a blueprint with a defined task family and customer segment.

## Important implementation status
The 1,000 entries are **role definitions only**. They are not 1,000 running AI models, autonomous workers, or income streams. The current JavaScript router is a basic keyword-routing prototype; it does not call an AI model or perform external work.

## Safe rollout
1. Build and test one central dispatcher.
2. Activate a small set of roles using a model provider, with usage limits and cost controls.
3. Run one real customer workflow end-to-end and measure delivery quality and profit.
4. Add user authentication, privacy safeguards, audit logs, rate limits, and human approval for external actions.
5. Expand only when demand and unit economics justify it.

## Revenue and payments
- Revenue targets are goals, not guarantees.
- Prefer selling real digital services to customers over speculative trading.
- No agent may autonomously trade, transfer funds, issue payouts, or purchase services.
- Never store bank card numbers, CVV, PINs, passwords, exchange credentials, or private keys in this repository.
- Payments must use a compliant payment provider and be reconciled after fees, refunds, taxes, and operating costs.

## Deployment
This registry is stored in GitHub only. It is not a deployed public product and is not connected to a live FLIXO website or Telegram bot.
